<Warning>
  Put the upload routes behind your own authentication before you deploy. The create route makes a video in your library and hands back a signature that lets the caller upload into it. Left open, anyone who finds the URL can fill your library with uploads that you pay to store, encode, and deliver.

  Server Actions and API routes are public HTTP endpoints, even when nothing in your UI links to them. Check the user on every request, and check that they own a video ID before you re-sign it or return its status.
</Warning>
