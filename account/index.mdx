The [Account Settings](https://dash.bunny.net/account/settings) page lets you manage your personal information, billing address, and company details.

## Personal details

Update your basic account information:

- **First name** (required)
- **Last name** (required)
- **Email** (required) - Your primary login email
- **Abuse report email** - Separate email for receiving abuse reports
- **Billing email** - Separate email for invoices and billing updates

<Note>
  If you configure a billing email, invoices will only be sent to that address
  and no longer to your main email.
</Note>

## Billing address

Your billing address appears on invoices. All fields are required:

- **Street address**
- **City**
- **ZIP code**
- **Country**

  ![Billing details

### Address verification

When you enter or update your billing address, we verify it against an international address database before it is saved. This keeps your invoices valid and ensures we apply the correct [tax rate](/billing/taxation).

We check that the address is real and that its parts are consistent with each other. If an address cannot be verified, it is not saved and you will see an error describing what did not match.

<Note>
  If your address is correct but still cannot be verified, [contact
  support](https://bunny.net/contact/) and we will review it manually.
</Note>

## When billing details changes appear on your invoice

Changes to your billing details apply from the next billing cycle, not the current one. The invoice for the current month still shows your previous details.

### Why

At the start of each month, we save your billing details and use them for all usage in that month. The invoice for that month, issued during the following month, shows those details.

A change made during the month doesn't affect the billing details already in use, so it applies from the next cycle.

### Example

You update your billing address on 15 July:

| Date | What happens | Details used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 July | You save the new billing address | — |
| 1 August | August cycle starts with your new details | New address |
| August | Invoice for July usage | Old address |
| September | Invoice for August usage | New address |

### Quick reference

| You change your details in… | First invoice with the new details |
| --- | --- |
| July | September |
| August | October |
| September | November |
