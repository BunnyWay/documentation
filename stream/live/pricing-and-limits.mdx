How live encoding is billed, and the limits that apply to live streams during the closed preview.

## Pricing

<Info>
  **Live encoding is free during the closed preview.** The rates below are the **proposed** pricing for general availability and may change before launch.
</Info>

Live encoding is billed **per minute**. The rate depends on the **top output resolution** of your live encode: the highest resolution under **Enabled resolutions** in your video library's [Encoding settings](/stream/live/ingest#customizing-the-abr-ladder-and-bitrates). It does not depend on the resolution of the feed your encoder sends, or on the ingest protocol you use.

| Top output resolution | Proposed price per minute |
| --- | --- |
| 1440p - 2160p / 4K | \$0.15 |
| 720p - 1080p | \$0.05 |
| 480p and below | \$0.025 |

For example, a library with a 720p top output is billed at the 1080p rate, and a library with a 360p top output is billed at the 480p rate.

### Primary and backup encodes

Bunny encodes the primary and backup ingest as two fully independent, parallel live encodes, so each one can take over instantly if the other fails. Because of this, **primary and backup are charged separately**. You only pay for a backup encode while a backup encoder is publishing.

For example, a 1-hour stream with a 1080p top output costs:

| Setup | Calculation | Live encoding cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary ingest only | 60 min × \$0.05 | \$3.00 |
| Primary + backup ingest | 2 × 60 min × \$0.05 | \$6.00 |

A Channel is billed the same way, for every minute an encoder is publishing to it. A Channel that runs 24/7 with a 1080p top output and primary ingest only costs 1,440 min × \$0.05 = \$72.00 per day.

### Other charges

Live encoding charges are in addition to your usual bunny.net usage:

- **CDN bandwidth** for viewers watching the live stream or the VOD recording.
- **Storage** for the VOD recording and any other files kept in your video library.

<Tip>
  To reduce live encoding costs, lower the top output resolution in your video library's **Encoding** settings to what your audience actually needs. Only run a backup encoder for broadcasts where uninterrupted delivery is worth the extra encode.
</Tip>

## Limits

- 12 hour maximum live duration for Events. Channels have no duration limit.
- 12 hour maximum DVR window for both Events and Channels.
- An Event ends after 30 minutes without a connected encoder, including when it was started by auto-start. A Channel stays open until you stop it.
- A Channel can't record a VOD.
- Only the part of a stream after it was started is recorded.
- 3 concurrent live streams per bunny account during closed preview, including auto-created streams.
- Auto-create resume window: `0` to `86400` seconds (24 hours), `1800` seconds (30 minutes) by default.
- **Startup delay.** The preview and the viewer playlist take 15-20 seconds to become available after the encoder connects and after the stream is started.
- **10,000 concurrent viewers**. There is no guaranteed SLA during the closed preview, and larger audiences may need up to **6 months' advance notice**. If you expect more than 10,000 concurrent viewers, [contact support](https://support.bunny.net) before your event.

## Known issues

These are known issues in the closed preview that we're working on:

- **`current-bitrate` may return `404`** while a stream is live. We're moving bitrate tracking to a new backend, which will fix this.
- **Two error formats.** Unknown IDs return `404` with a `problem+json` body, while state errors return `400` with a `{"success": false, ...}` body. See [Errors](/stream/live/api#errors).
- **The `public` field has no effect.** Setting it to `false` doesn't restrict playback. Use your video library's security settings to control access instead.

## Feature support

Some Bunny Stream features available for VOD (video on-demand) are not yet supported in Stream Live during the closed preview. However, once a live stream ends and is recorded as a VOD, it will gain support for those features - as long as they are enabled in your video library settings. 

| Feature | VOD | Stream Live |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Multi-audio](/stream/multi-audio) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| [Transcription](/stream/transcribing) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| [Captions](/stream/video-specification#caption-support) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| [MediaCage Basic DRM](/stream/mediacage-basic) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| [MediaCage Enterprise DRM](/stream/mediacage-enterprise) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| [Premium Encoding codecs](/stream/premium-encoding#output-codecs): x265 (HEVC), AV1, VP9 | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported. Live output is H.264 only. |
| [10-bit HDR](/stream/premium-encoding#multiple-output-codec-support) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported. Live output is 8-bit SDR only. Convert HDR sources to SDR before you stream. See [Color and HDR](/stream/live/ffmpeg-encoding-guide#color-and-hdr). |
| [VAST ad insertion](/stream/vast) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| [Content tagging](/stream/tagging) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| [Statistics](/stream/statistics) | ✅ Supported | ❌ Not supported |
| Low-latency HLS | Not applicable | ❌ Not supported |
| VOD clipping | ❌ Not supported | ❌ Not supported |
| SRT Ingest | Not applicable | ✅ Supported |
| SRT Output | Not applicable | ❌ Not supported |
