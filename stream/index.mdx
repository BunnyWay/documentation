![bunny.net Stream](/images/stream/stream.png)

Bunny Stream is a video platform that takes care of the whole pipeline for you: upload a file, and we encode it into adaptive streams, replicate it to the storage regions you choose, and deliver it over bunny.net's global CDN to a player you can embed on any website or app.

Use it to power course platforms, media sites, SaaS products, or any app that needs reliable video playback without running your own encoding and delivery infrastructure.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a video library">
    A video library holds your videos together with their player, encoding, and security settings. When you create one, you choose which storage regions your videos are [replicated](/stream/replication) to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your videos">
    Upload from the [dashboard](/stream/dashboard), the [HTTP API](/stream/http-api), a [remote URL](/stream/url-fetch), or with [TUS resumable uploads](/stream/tus-resumable-uploads) for large files and unreliable connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We encode and store them">
    Each video is transcoded into multiple resolutions for [adaptive bitrate streaming](/stream/adaptive-bitrate), then stored in every region you enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver and play anywhere">
    Videos are served from bunny.net's CDN and play in [Bunny Player](/stream/player), a [third-party player](/stream/players), or your own [mobile app](/stream/mobile-sdk).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Build with Stream

Integrate Bunny Stream into your stack with the [Stream API](/stream/api-reference). Start with [Authentication](/stream/authentication) to find your library's API key, then pick a guide for your platform:

| Platform | Guides |
| --- | --- |
| Web player | [React](/stream/player/react), [Next.js](/stream/player/nextjs), [Vue and Nuxt](/stream/player/vue), [Svelte and SvelteKit](/stream/player/svelte), [Astro](/stream/player/astro), [JavaScript](/stream/player/javascript) |
| Mobile | [Android](/stream/android-sdk), [iOS](/stream/ios-sdk), [React Native](/stream/react-native-sdk), [Expo](/stream/expo-video) |
| Server-side uploads | [Laravel](/stream/upload/laravel), [Rails](/stream/upload/rails), [Django](/stream/upload/django) |
| Third-party players | [Bitmovin](/stream/players/bitmovin), [Shaka Player](/stream/players/shaka), [Widevine HTML5](/stream/players/widevine), [FairPlay HTML5](/stream/players/fairplay) |
| Integrations | [WordPress](/integrations/wordpress/stream), [Terraform](/integrations/terraform/importing-stream-library) |

<Note>
  Moving from Vimeo? The [Vimeo2Bunny CLI](/stream/vimeo2bunny) helps you migrate your existing videos to Bunny Stream.
</Note>
