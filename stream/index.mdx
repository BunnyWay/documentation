![bunny.net Stream](/images/stream/stream.png)

Bunny Stream is a video platform that takes care of the whole pipeline for you: upload a file, and we encode it into adaptive streams, replicate it to the storage regions you choose, and deliver it over bunny.net's global CDN to a player you can embed on any website or app.

Use it to power course platforms, media sites, SaaS products, or any app that needs reliable video playback without running your own encoding and delivery infrastructure.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a video library">
    A video library holds your videos together with their player, encoding, and security settings. When you create one, you choose which storage regions your videos are [replicated](/stream/replication) to globally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your videos">
    Upload from the dashboard, the API, a remote URL, or directly from your users' browsers and mobile apps with resumable uploads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We encode and store them">
    Each video is transcoded into multiple resolutions for [adaptive bitrate streaming](/stream/adaptive-bitrate). <br />With [Premium Encoding](/stream/premium-encoding), videos are typically playable within seconds of upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver and play anywhere">
    Videos are served from bunny.net's CDN and play in [Bunny Player](/stream/player), a [third-party player](/stream/players), or a native [mobile app](/stream/mobile-sdk).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Premium add-ons

Turn on paid add-ons for a library when you need faster processing, wider reach, or stronger protection. Each is billed on top of storage and delivery. See [Pricing](/stream/pricing) for rates.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Premium Encoding" icon="zap" href="/stream/premium-encoding" cta="Explore Premium Encoding">
    Make most videos playable in 10–15 seconds with Just-in-Time encoding, skip the queue with prioritized encoding, and output VP9, HEVC, and AV1 with HDR.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI transcribing" icon="captions" href="/stream/transcribing" cta="Explore transcribing">
    Generate captions automatically, translate them into 56 languages, and create titles, descriptions, and chapters with Smart Generate.
  </Card>

  <Card title="MediaCage Enterprise DRM" icon="lock-keyhole" href="/stream/mediacage-enterprise" cta="Explore Enterprise DRM">
    Widevine and FairPlay protection with screen capture prevention, native app support, and custom player integration, built for premium and pay-per-view content.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Geo-replication" icon="globe" href="/stream/replication" cta="Explore geo-replication">
    Replicate videos to additional storage regions for faster uncached delivery and automatic failover during regional outages.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
