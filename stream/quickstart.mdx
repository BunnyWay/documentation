Getting a video onto your own page takes three steps in the dashboard: create a library, upload the file, and copy the embed code. You need a [bunny.net account](https://dash.bunny.net/auth/register) and a video file.

## Create a video library

A video library holds your videos along with their player, encoding, and security settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Stream">
    In the [dashboard](https://dash.bunny.net), go to **Delivery** > **Stream** and click **Create Your first Video Library**.

    <Frame>
      ![Stream page in the dashboard with the Create Your first Video Library button highlighted](/images/docs/6295ff2-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the library">
    Enter a name you'll recognise in the dashboard.

    <Frame>
      ![Video Library Name field on the Add Video Library page](/images/docs/31a980e-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose storage regions">
    We replicate your videos to every region you enable. Pick at least two to keep a copy if one region has a hardware failure, and one per continent where your viewers are for the fastest uncached delivery.

    <Frame>
      ![Video Storage Configuration with a map and a list of storage regions](/images/docs/34c369a-image.png)
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      Replication regions can't be removed once they're enabled.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Add Video Library**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload a video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the uploader">
    In your new library, click **Upload a Video**.

    <Frame>
      ![Manage Videos page with the Upload a Video button highlighted](/images/docs/915e570-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your file">
    Drag the file into the dialog or click **Browse**. The upload starts straight away.

    <Frame>
      ![Upload Video File dialog with a drag and drop area and the supported formats](/images/docs/3e63ff3-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for encoding">
    We encode the video into several resolutions once the upload reaches 100%, so it plays smoothly on any device and connection.

    <Frame>
      ![File Upload panel showing a finished upload at 100%](/images/docs/284350f-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share or embed it

To share a link, open the video's menu and click **Copy Video Play Url**. The link opens the video in Bunny Player.

<Frame>
  ![Video menu with Copy Video Play Url highlighted](/images/docs/9171444-image.png)
</Frame>

To put the video on your own page, open it and copy the code from the **Embed** panel. The toggles above it set autoplay, looping, muting, and preloading for that embed.

<Frame>
  ![Video page with the Embed panel highlighted, showing the embed options and code](/images/docs/cbfbc9a-image.png)
</Frame>

Paste the code into your page's HTML where the player should appear. [Embedding videos](/stream/embedding) covers every player parameter and the embedding restrictions you can set on a library.
