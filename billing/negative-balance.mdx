---
title: Negative Balance
description: What happens when your bunny.net balance reaches zero, which charges continue, and how to restore your account.
---

bunny.net is a prepaid service. Charges are deducted from your balance as you use services. If your balance runs out, usage keeps being charged and your balance becomes negative. You remain responsible for all charges on your account, including those made while the balance is negative.

<Note>
  Enable [Auto-Recharge](/billing/auto-recharge) to top up automatically before
  your balance runs out.
</Note>

## Why a balance can go negative

Usage is billed after it happens, in short intervals. If you use more between two billing runs than your remaining balance covers, the balance goes below zero. Charges for stored data, such as Storage and Stream storage, continue for as long as the data exists.

## What happens when your balance reaches zero

1. **You are notified.** We email you daily while your balance is negative, and the dashboard shows a warning.
2. **Auto-Recharge runs**, if enabled. The payment covers your recharge amount plus the negative amount, so your balance returns to the amount you chose.
3. **You can't create new resources** until your balance is positive again.
4. **Your account is disabled** if the balance is not topped up within 4 days. We may disable an account sooner, for example for new accounts or large negative amounts.

## What happens when your account is disabled

All your resources stop serving. Your files and configuration are kept, and you can still log in to the dashboard to top up.

Usage charges stop because the services stop. Charges for stored data continue, and the [\$1 minimum monthly usage](/billing/add-funds#minimum-monthly-usage) still applies, so your balance can keep decreasing while the account is disabled. To stop storage charges, delete the storage zones or video libraries you no longer need.

## Restoring your account

Top up enough to bring your balance to zero or above. Your account and services are re-enabled automatically within a few minutes. The negative amount is deducted from the top-up. For example, with a balance of -\$4, a \$10 top-up leaves \$6.

## Data deletion

If your account stays disabled for 60 days, all stored data is permanently deleted. This includes storage zones, video libraries, Magic Containers apps and volumes, and database content. Deleted data can't be recovered.

We email you 30 days before deletion, again 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before, and on the day of deletion.
