The [Billing History](https://dash.bunny.net/account/billing-history) page shows all your account transactions, including monthly usage, payments, and applied promo codes.

<Frame>
  ![Billing history](/images/accounts-and-billing/billing-history.png)
</Frame>

## What you'll see

The billing history displays:

- **Date** of the transaction
- **Description** of the charge or credit
- **Amount**
- **Type** (Monthly usage, PayPal, Coupon code, etc.)

A chart at the top shows your account balance over time.

## Receipts and invoices

bunny.net issues two kinds of billing documents:

- **Receipts** confirm a payment. You get a receipt each time you add funds, either manually or through [Auto-Recharge](/billing/auto-recharge).
- **Invoices** are issued once a month and cover the previous month's usage. Tax is charged on usage, not on the funds you add, so any VAT or sales tax appears on your monthly invoice. See [Taxation](/billing/taxation).

<Note>
  Adding funds does not create an invoice. For your accounting records, use the
  monthly invoice for the month in which the usage happened.
</Note>

## Downloading receipts

For payment entries, click **Download receipt** to get a PDF receipt. We also email the receipt to you after each payment.

Payments made before your account moved to monthly invoicing may show **Download invoice** instead.

## Downloading monthly invoices

At the start of each month, we issue an invoice for the previous month's usage and email it to you. To download it, click **Download invoice** on the monthly usage entry for that month.

No invoice is issued for a month with no charges.

## Downloading usage summaries

For monthly usage entries, click **Download summary** to get a detailed breakdown of your charges for that period.

The usage summary includes:

- Your billing details
- Starting and ending balance
- Total activity, credits, payments, and refunds
- Itemized usage by service (traffic by region, storage, scripting, etc.)

<Note>
  Usage summaries are not invoices. They are a detailed breakdown of billing
  activity for the stated period.
</Note>
