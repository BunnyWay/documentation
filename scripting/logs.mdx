---
title: "Logs"
description: "See the messages and errors your script writes while it runs."
---

Logs record the messages, errors, and other runtime information your script produces, which is usually the quickest way to work out why it behaved the way it did.

To view the logs, select the **Logs** tab in the navigation pane:
