<Update label="2026-08-04" tags={["New"]}>
  ## CDN-Bot header for verified bot detection

  Bunny Shield now forwards bot detection results to your origin in the `CDN-Bot` request header. For requests that match a known bot, the header includes the bot name, category, verification result, and applied action, letting your origin make its own decisions based on bot identity without re-implementing detection. [Learn more](/shield/verified-bots#forwarding-bot-information-to-your-origin)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-30" tags={["New"]}>
  ## Verified bot customization

  You can now control how Bunny Shield handles verified bots per Shield Zone — allow, block, or take no action — with per-bot and per-category overrides across seven categories: SEO, AI Scraper, AI Tool, Tool, Ads, Preview, and Social. Verification supports both reverse DNS and published IP range checks. Verified bot categories are also exposed to the Rule Engine via the `VERIFIED_BOT_CATEGORY` variable, enabling custom WAF rules that key off verified bot identity. [Learn more](/shield/verified-bots)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-06-23" tags={["New"]}>
  ## Custom Response Pages

  Customize the challenge, block, and rate limit pages shown to visitors when Bunny Shield takes action. Replace default response pages with fully branded HTML experiences that match your website, including custom styling, messaging, and support information. Available on Advanced and higher Shield plans. [Learn more](/shield/custom-response-pages)

</Update>

<Update label="2026-04-27" tags={["New"]}>
  ## API Guardian

Introducing API Guardian, a new feature in Bunny Shield that provides schema-aware protection for your APIs. It enforces your OpenAPI contract at the edge, ensuring requests and responses match your application's expectations, and stops invalid or abusive traffic before it reaches your origin. [Learn more](/shield/api-guardian)

</Update>
