---
title: "Limitations"
description: "Current limits of the bunny.net Partner Portal while it is in early access."
---

The Partner Portal is in early access, and these limits apply to every partner account.

| Limitation | Details |
| --- | --- |
| One account owner | The Partner Portal is limited to a single bunny.net account owner. |
| Billing details and MFA required | The partner account must have billing details and [multi-factor authentication](/account/two-factor-authentication) set up before you can sign in. |
| No account-wide pricing | Pricing is set for each customer individually, so there is no partner-wide price list to apply across your customers. |
| Your own account can't be a customer | You can't add your partner account as one of your customers. The portal shows a summary of its usage. |
| Customer account access is MSP only | Only [MSP Partners](/partners/partner-types#msp-partner) can open a customer's dashboard. |

If one of these blocks your setup, talk to your bunny.net partner contact or [our support team](https://bunny.net/contact/).
