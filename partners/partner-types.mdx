The Partner Portal is available to two types of partner account: **Reseller Partner** and **MSP Partner**. Both manage their customers from the same portal. The difference is how much access you have to each customer's account.

| Capability                                          | Reseller | MSP |
| --------------------------------------------------- | :------: | :-: |
| Create and invite customers                         |    ✓     |  ✓  |
| View contract, pricing, and billing                 |    ✓     |  ✓  |
| Review a customer's audit log                       |    ✓     |  ✓  |
| [Access a customer's account](#msp-partner)         |          |  ✓  |

## Reseller Partner

Reseller Partners manage customers from the Partner Portal only. You can see each customer's usage, contract, and billing, but you can't sign in to their bunny.net dashboard.

## MSP Partner

MSP Partners can also open a customer's bunny.net dashboard from the Partner Portal, to configure their setup or help them troubleshoot. Use **Access account** from the menu on the customer's row, or from the top of their contract page.

## What your customers see

Your customers sign in to the standard bunny.net dashboard, with billing disabled. They can't sign in to the Partner Portal.
