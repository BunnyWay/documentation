The Partner Portal supports two types of partner account, Reseller Partner and MSP Partner. Both use the same portal to manage customers, and MSP Partners can also open a customer's bunny.net dashboard.

| Capability                                          | Reseller | MSP |
| --------------------------------------------------- | :------: | :-: |
| Create and invite customers                         |   Yes    | Yes |
| View contract, pricing, and billing                 |   Yes    | Yes |
| Review a customer's audit log                       |   Yes    | Yes |
| [Access a customer's account](/partners/access-a-customer-account) |    No    | Yes |

## Reseller Partner

Reseller Partners work from the Partner Portal, where you can see each customer's usage, contract, pricing, and billing. Opening a customer's bunny.net dashboard is limited to MSP Partners.

## MSP Partner

MSP Partners get everything a Reseller Partner has, plus access to each customer's bunny.net dashboard. Use it to configure their setup or help them troubleshoot, as described in [Access a customer account](/partners/access-a-customer-account).

## What your customers see

Your customers sign in to the standard bunny.net dashboard with billing disabled. The Partner Portal is for partner accounts only.
