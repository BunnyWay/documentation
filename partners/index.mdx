![bunny.net Partner Portal](/images/partners/bunny-partners.png)

The Partner Portal is where bunny.net partners manage the accounts they look after. Add customers, then keep an eye on their usage, contract, pricing, billing, and account activity from one place.

<Note>
  The Partner Portal is available to partners only. [Contact us](https://bunny.net/contact/) if you would like to join the partner program.
</Note>

## What you can manage

| Area      | Description                                                                          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Customers | Create new customer accounts, or invite existing bunny.net users to become customers  |
| Pricing   | Review your partner pricing, and the effective prices and overrides for each customer |
| Contract  | Review the contract details for your partner account                                  |
| Billing   | View a customer's billing summary and invoice history, and download invoice PDFs      |
| Audit logs | Review the actions taken on a customer's account, by date                            |

## Add a customer

Create a new bunny.net account for your customer, or invite an account they already have. Both work in the portal and through the API, and [Add a customer](/partners/add-a-customer) walks through each one.

## Automate with the API

The Partner Portal API covers the same customers, pricing, and billing data, so you can sync customers with your own systems, pull billing into your invoicing, or create accounts when someone signs up with you.
