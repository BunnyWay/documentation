Bunny Optimizer is priced at \$9.50 per Pull Zone per month and includes unlimited optimisation, requests, and transformations. CDN bandwidth and Burrow usage are billed separately.

For details on Burrow usage pricing, see [Burrow pricing](/optimizer/burrow-smart-routing#burrow-pricing).
